LifeCare Form Builder V19 — Upload control polish

Tests: 35/35 existing Node tests passed; existing Chromium mobile editor and respondent smoke tests passed; attachment control and QR upload visibility checks passed. Three generated QR crops decoded using OpenCV to the expected demo URL. Live Netlify deployment, actual upload against live Netlify and phone scanning not tested.
