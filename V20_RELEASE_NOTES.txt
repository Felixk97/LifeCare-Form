LifeCare Form Builder V20

Updates in this version:
- Reworked the “Artistic” QR option into a sample-inspired “Brand Artistic” style.
- Artistic QR now uses logo-art styling, stronger artwork fill, custom decorative corner circles and centred logo handling.
- Artistic QR automatically reuses the uploaded artwork or selected centre logo when available.
- QR customization panel labels updated to better describe the artistic style.
- Existing fixed LifeCare poster background remains unchanged.
- All existing form-builder, screening, response-sheet and attachment features retained.

Notes:
- The brand-artistic QR is sample-inspired, not a pixel-for-pixel copy of the sample brands.
- The design still prioritizes scannability, so the generator may reduce the logo size when needed.
